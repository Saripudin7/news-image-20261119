# news-image-20261119 — The World Today: Thursday, 19 November 2026

A one-image summary of the world's news of Thursday 19 November 2026:
the Trump–Xi–Putin summit in Shenzhen, the Crimean Bridge attack, Israel's
ground operation in Gaza, Anthropic's record $81.3bn IPO, the Van Gogh
record sale of $190.4m, and Voyager 1 reaching one light-day from Earth.

The infographic is drawn from scratch with Pillow (`draw_news.py`) and
`main.py` copies the resulting `entry.png` to the output directory.
